## Decision

**What we will do:** Implement Adaptive AV Coverage Model with three-layer structure

**Framework Components:**
1. **Base Layer:** Traditional liability coverage for human-driven segments ($0.15/mile)
2. **Technology Layer:** Real-time telematics risk adjustment (±30% monthly)
3. **Catastrophic Layer:** Systemic risk protection ($0.03/mile flat rate)

**Why:**
- Balances actuarial rigor with AV innovation requirements
- Addresses both gradual risk transfer and tail catastrophic scenarios
- Provides feasible regulatory pathway through phased implementation

**Implementation Timeline:**
- **Phase 1 (6 months):** Basic telematics integration
- **Phase 2 (18 months):** Dynamic adjustments + manufacturer partnerships
- **Phase 3 (36 months):** Full catastrophic modeling with regulatory approval
